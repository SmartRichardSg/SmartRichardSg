📖 Smart Richard User Guide

A few simple things to know before you start.

1

📱 Put Smart Richard on your Home Screen

You do not need to save an image of the icon. When you add this website to your Home Screen using your browser's built-in option, the Smart Richard icon will be used automatically.

🍎 iPhone / iPad — Safari

  1. Open Smart Richard in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Check that the Smart Richard name and icon are shown.
  5. Tap Add.

🤖 Android — Chrome

  1. Open Smart Richard in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, depending on your phone.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions and tap Add or Install.

💻 Windows — Chrome

  1. Open Smart Richard in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu ⋮.
  3. Choose Save and share → Create shortcut (wording may vary).
  4. Name it Smart Richard, then choose Create.

💻 Windows — Edge

  1. Open Smart Richard in Edge.
  2. Open the ⋯ menu.
  3. Choose Apps, then Install this site as an app when available.
  4. Follow the instructions to install it.
⭐ Why do this? Once the icon is on your Home Screen or desktop, you can open Smart Richard like an ordinary app without searching for the website again.
2

🔒 Where is my information saved?

Many Smart Richard apps are designed to save activities and progress on the device you are using.

Important: Information saved on one device does not necessarily appear automatically on another phone, tablet or computer.

Each app may handle information differently. Read the app's own instructions where provided.

3

💾 Make a backup when your information matters

If an app provides an Export, Backup or Download function, use it from time to time for information you do not want to lose.

Keep an important backup somewhere separate from the device when practical.

4

📁 Where can I find a saved file?

🍎 iPhone / iPad

Open the Files app and check Downloads or the folder you selected when saving.

🤖 Android

Open Files, My Files or your device's file manager and check Downloads.

💻 Computer

Check your browser's Downloads folder, or the location you selected when saving.

5

📱 Using an app on another device

If an app stores information locally, your records may stay on the original device. They may not appear automatically on a new phone or computer.

If the app provides an Export/Import or Backup/Restore option, use that to move your information.

6

⚠️ Important things to remember

  • Do not clear browser data for an app unless you understand what may be removed.
  • Do not assume that local information is backed up automatically.
  • If your device is lost, damaged or reset, information stored only on that device may be lost.
  • For important records, make a backup when the app provides a way to do so.
7

❓ Need help?

If something does not work as expected, comments and suggestions are welcome. They help improve the apps for everyone.

A direct contact email will be added when the Smart Richard contact address is set up.

🌱 One final thought

You do not need to learn everything at once. Learn one useful thing, use it, and build your confidence one small step at a time.
